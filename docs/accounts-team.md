# Your HK Planner account

You now sign in to HK Planner with your own email address and your own password.
Your PIN still works on the phones where you already use it, but the email account
is what a manager sets up for you from now on.

## Getting your account

1. A manager adds your email address in HK Planner and taps Invite.
2. You get an email from "HK Planner" with the subject "Your HK Planner account is ready".
3. Tap "Set your password" in that email. HK Planner opens.
4. Choose a password of at least 8 characters, type it twice, tap "Save password".

That is it. You are signed in.

The link in the email works once and expires after 1 hour. If it expired, open
HK Planner, tap "Forgot password?" and get a fresh one.

## Signing in later

Open HK Planner, type your email address and your password, tap "Sign in".
You stay signed in on that phone until you sign out, so you normally do this once
per phone.

## Forgot your password

1. Open HK Planner. If you are on the PIN screen, tap "Sign in with email".
2. Type your email address.
3. Tap "Forgot password?".
4. You get an email with a link. Tap it, choose a new password, done.

If no email arrives, check your spam folder first. If it still does not arrive,
ask a manager: your email address may be misspelled in HK Planner.

## Good to know

- Nobody can see your password, not even a manager. A manager can only send you
  a new link to choose a new one.
- Use your own account, never a colleague's. Everything you do in the app is
  recorded under your name: cleanings marked done, tickets, laundry counts, leave.
- On a shared phone, sign out when you are done (the Logout button at the top of
  the Planner screen). Signing out also stops the notifications on that phone.
- If you change phones, sign in with the same email and password on the new one.
  Then turn notifications back on (see "Turning on HK Planner notifications").

## For managers: inviting someone

1. Settings, panel "Team Members".
2. Type the person's email address on their line, tap "Invite".
   For a new person: fill Name, Phone, Email, Role, then tap "Add & invite".
3. If the person already has an account, the button says "Resend" and sends them
   a password reset link instead. That is the right button in both cases.
4. Someone without an email address (no mailbox) is still created with
   "Add (PIN only)" and keeps signing in with a PIN.

Removing someone from the team deletes their account: they can no longer sign in,
on any phone.
