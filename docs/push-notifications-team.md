# Turning on HK Planner notifications

You will get a notification on your phone when a task or a cleaning is assigned to you,
even when the app is closed. Two steps, once per phone.

## Step 1: install HK Planner on your Home Screen

On iPhone this step is required. Notifications do not work from Safari.

1. Open https://stunning-kleicha-f61101.netlify.app in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
4. Close Safari. From now on, always open HK Planner from the new icon.

On Android, open the same link in Chrome and tap "Install app" when Chrome offers it.
It is not strictly required, but notifications are more reliable once installed.

## Step 2: turn notifications on

1. Open HK Planner from the Home Screen icon and log in with your PIN.
2. On the Planner screen, tap "Enable" in the notification banner at the top.
3. Your phone asks for permission. Tap "Allow".

That is it. Ask a manager to send you a test notification to confirm it works.

## What you will receive

- A new task assigned to you, with its title and who created it.
- A new cleaning assigned to you, with the date and the type of service.

Tap the notification to open HK Planner on the right screen.

## Good to know

- Between 22:00 and 08:30 Dubai time, only urgent tasks send a notification. Everything
  else waits for you in the app, so check HK Planner when you start your day.
- If you log out, notifications stop on that phone. Log back in and tap Enable again.
- If you delete the Home Screen icon, you have to redo both steps.
- Notifications never contain guest names or apartment numbers. Open the app for details.

## Nothing arrives?

1. Open HK Planner from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari or Chrome.
2. Close the app completely (swipe it away), then open it again. This reloads the
   background worker that receives the notifications.
3. Check your phone settings: Settings, Notifications, HK Planner, Allow Notifications on.
4. In HK Planner, open Settings and check that notifications show as on. If not, tap
   Enable again.
5. Still nothing: tell a manager. They can send you a test notification and see whether
   your phone is registered.
